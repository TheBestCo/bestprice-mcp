## Version and scope limits

The service version is `1.8.0`. The package version is independent and is now
`1.2.0`; neither number identifies deployed source. Future service releases need
their own exact-revision observation.

This 47-check profile is not the full live 31-scenario / 62-response staff Ask–MCP
sweep. It also does not attest directory review or publication status. The explicit
checkout and catalog-export Brain refusals were checked separately at compatible
reader revision `cd349685d585f1f34915194a872b192a68e81fc4`, with zero searches;
they are not silently counted as additional Brain prompts in this artifact.

Known catalog limits remain explicit: missing OIS evidence on the checked Galaxy
A56 records still produces an honest no-match. Repeated grouped-optionality catalog
checks with fixed readings do not establish that the historical intermittent empty
upstream response is eliminated. A passing bounded canary is not an uptime guarantee.
