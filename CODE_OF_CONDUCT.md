# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

We expect everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, and discussions, to be respectful and constructive,
to accept feedback gracefully, and to focus on what is best for the people who rely on this software.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing others' private information are not
acceptable. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not follow this code and may block
repeat offenders.

Report unacceptable behaviour to [feedback@bestprice.gr](mailto:feedback@bestprice.gr). Reports are handled
confidentially by The Best Company S.A.
